FAQ

Common Questions

The FAQ layout uses independent columns so opening one answer does not enlarge the neighbouring card.

Who controls the hotel data?

The hotel controls its Google Workspace account and determines which accounts, documents and permissions are used.

Can the workflow be customized?

Yes. Room types, booking sources, reports and automation rules can be adapted to the property's processes.

Is every AI result accepted automatically?

No. Unclear or incomplete information can be flagged for manual confirmation.

Do we need new hotel software?

Not necessarily. The workflow can build on Gmail, Google Sheets, Drive and Calendar.
