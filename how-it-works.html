How It Works

Automation With Human Oversight

Repetitive information can move automatically through the workflow while incomplete or uncertain cases remain visible to hotel staff.

A Typical Booking Workflow

The exact workflow can be adapted to the booking sources and processes used by each property.

1

Booking Email Arrives

A booking confirmation or booking request arrives in the hotel's Gmail account.

2

Relevant Information Is Extracted

The system identifies stay dates, guest details, room type, price, number of guests and booking source.

3

Information Is Validated

Basic checks can identify missing fields, inconsistent values or possible duplicate bookings.

4

Unclear Cases Are Flagged

Information that cannot be interpreted reliably remains visible for manual confirmation.

5

Hotel Records Are Updated

Validated information can update connected booking, guest, room and revenue records.

6

Daily Operations Stay Visible

Staff can review upcoming arrivals, departures, open payments and operational issues from a clearer overview.

Human control

Automation Does Not Have to Mean Blind Automation

A useful hotel workflow should reduce repeated data entry without hiding uncertainty.

When a booking email is incomplete or ambiguous, the system can mark the relevant information instead of silently inventing a value.

Human review can remain required for

  • Unclear room categories
  • Missing prices
  • Suspicious dates
  • Potential double bookings
  • Unusual payment situations
  • Incomplete guest information
