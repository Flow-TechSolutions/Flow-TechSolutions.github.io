Data & Security

Keep Control of Your Hotel Records

The workflow is designed around accounts, permissions and documents controlled by the hotel rather than hiding important records inside an opaque system.

1

Your Accounts

The hotel retains control over the Google accounts used by the workflow.

2

Your Documents

Booking sheets, calendars and operational records remain accessible through the hotel's own tools.

3

Controlled Permissions

Access should be limited to the accounts and services required for the configured workflow.

4

Human Review

Uncertain AI-generated information can be marked for verification instead of automatically being treated as correct.

5

Automation Can Be Stopped

Automation rules can be modified or disabled without requiring the underlying hotel records to disappear.

6

Clear Structure

Operational information remains visible in understandable documents rather than depending completely on a black-box interface.

Important principle

Automation Should Not Mean Losing Access to Your Data

If an automation is disabled, the hotel should still be able to access the underlying sheets, calendars and records used for day-to-day operations.

Practical controls

  • Property-controlled Google account
  • Explicit document permissions
  • Reviewable automation rules
  • Human confirmation where required
  • Accessible operational records

Questions About Your Specific Setup?

Permissions and workflow architecture depend on the services and accounts used by the individual property.
