Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about setup, customization, data ownership and AI-assisted workflows.

What happens if the service is stopped?

Your underlying documents and records remain in your own Google account. Disabling an automation does not automatically remove booking sheets, calendars or historical information.

Is every AI result accepted automatically?

No. Unclear or incomplete information can be marked for manual confirmation before it is treated as final.

Are multiple properties supported?

Multiple properties can be supported through a custom setup with separate records, permissions and reporting structures.

Is staff training available?

Remote onboarding and staff guidance can be included for Professional and Custom setups.

Who controls the hotel data?

The hotel controls its Google Workspace account and determines which accounts, documents and permissions are used for the setup.

Can the workflow be customized?

Yes. Room types, booking sources, reporting fields, payment tracking and automation rules can be adapted to the property's processes.

How long does setup take?

A basic setup can usually be prepared within several working days. More complex workflows depend on the number of rooms, booking sources and required reports.

Do we need new hotel software?

Not necessarily. The service can build on familiar Google Workspace tools instead of requiring a complete replacement of the hotel's existing workflow.
