# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, professional, and welcoming environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behavior

Participants should:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively
- Accept differing opinions and technical perspectives
- Provide useful and professional feedback
- Focus discussions on the project and its development
- Respect the time and work of other contributors

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or insulting comments
- Deliberate disruption of discussions
- Publishing private information without permission
- Spam or unrelated promotional content

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate these standards.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from participation in the project.
